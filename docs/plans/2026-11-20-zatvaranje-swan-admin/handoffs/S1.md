# S1: mjerenje Monerium inboxa + guardovi na `intent_settlements`

Polazni commit: (upisuje S0). `npm test`: 889 zelenih (2026-11-20, `cd backend && npx vitest run`). Odluke od **100**.

Kontekst: PR #155 (mig 0036) uveo je `intent_settlements` (sid PK, provider, settlement_ref,
credit_event_id), koji se piše u ISTOM D1 batchu kao `payment_intents.monerium_order_id`
(`backend/src/intents/db.ts`, `runSettlementClaim` u `markIntentPaid/Underpaid/Late`). Backfill u prodi
217 redova (156 monerium + 57 sandbox + 4 swan). Monerium webhook (`monerium/webhookHandler.ts` →
`monerium/inbox.ts`) zapisuje issue ordere u `credit_events` (pending → notified, processed → booked,
providerRef `<orderId>:<finality>`), ali ne pogoni ništa. Vidi ADR 0032, sekcija „Stanje 2026-11-18”.

Paket:

1. **Mjerenje (samo čitanje prode)**:
   ```
   cd backend
   npx wrangler d1 execute pay_domovina --remote --command "SELECT provider_ref, finality, matched_sid, match_note, created_at FROM credit_events WHERE provider='monerium' ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 10"
   npx wrangler d1 execute pay_domovina --remote --command "SELECT s.sid, s.provider, s.credit_event_id FROM intent_settlements s JOIN payment_intents i USING(sid) WHERE s.provider='monerium' AND i.paid_at > '2026-11-18T14:30' ORDER BY i.paid_at DESC LIMIT 10"
   npx wrangler d1 execute pay_domovina --remote --command "SELECT COUNT(*) FROM payment_intents i WHERE (i.monerium_order_id IS NOT NULL) <> EXISTS(SELECT 1 FROM intent_settlements s WHERE s.sid=i.sid)"
   ```
   Zadnji upit mora dati 0. Ako nema nijedne Monerium uplate nakon 18. 11. 14:30, napravi sandbox
   uplatu (`/api/intents/<sid>/monerium-sandbox-transfer` na sandbox/staging tenantu, ne `italk`).
   Brojke u `research/2026-11-2x-monerium-inbox-mjerenje.md`. Ako booked događaj nije vezan
   (`credit_event_id` NULL), to je nalaz: zapiši ga na vrh `S2.md` i u odluke; S4 ne smije krenuti.
2. **Guardovi** (grana `refactor/intent-settlements-guards`): svako `monerium_order_id IS NULL` /
   `IS NOT NULL` koje znači „intent je namiren” prebaci na (ne)postojanje reda u `intent_settlements`:
   - `intents/db.ts` claimovi (~r. 231, 270) — paziti: claim i insert u `intent_settlements` su u istom
     batchu, pa `NOT EXISTS` mora vidjeti stanje prije inserta (redoslijed naredbi u batchu);
   - `intents/db.ts` `findStrayCandidates` (~r. 367, 406), `intents/shortRef.ts` (~r. 166);
   - `intents/list.ts` bucketi (~r. 106–108), `payers/api.ts` (~r. 90);
   - provjeri i `intents/intentStates.ts`, `inflow/matcher.ts` (`settleable`) — `grep -rn monerium_order_id backend/src`.
   Mjesta gdje `monerium_order_id` služi kao ID Monerium ordera (join na `monerium_orders`) ostaju.
3. **Test usklađenosti**: novi test koji za paid, underpaid, late, sandbox i swan namiru provjerava
   da `monerium_order_id IS NOT NULL` ⇔ postoji red u `intent_settlements`, i da drugi pokušaj namire
   (dvostruki webhook) ne prolazi.

Rizik: pogrešan redoslijed u D1 batchu = claim koji uvijek prolazi (dvostruka namira) ili nikad.
